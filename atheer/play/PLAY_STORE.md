**Full description — English:**
“Our Kids Games” is a joyful learning world for children aged 3–8, with more than 45 games in Arabic and English — fully offline and with no ads.

🔤 Language: trace Arabic & English letters, vowel race, word train, letter puzzles, opposites and a magic writing pad.
🔢 Math: counting, tower building, penguins, abacus, balance scale, cake fractions, peg board and a piggy bank to learn money.
🧠 Thinking: shape matching, memory, mazes, spot the difference, patterns, shadows, stacking rings, blocks, keys & locks.
🌍 Science: body parts, jobs, seasons & weather, life cycles, animal mothers and a map of Sudan.
🕌 Values: wudu & prayer, little duas, road safety and interactive stories with different endings.
🎮 Fun: cloud knight, funny taxi, bubble shooter and more.

For parents: a profile for each child with name and photo, real child voice narration, an in-app recording studio, no ads, no purchases, no data collection — everything stays on the device.
